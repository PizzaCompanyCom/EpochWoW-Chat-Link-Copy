WoW Chat Copy & Links v1.1.4
World of Warcraft 3.3.5a / TrinityCore

INSTALLATION
Extract the WoWChatCopyLinks folder into Interface\AddOns\ and restart the game.

QUICK GUIDE
- Click the minimap icon or type /chatcopy (or /ccl) to open chat history.
- Drag the minimap icon to move it.
- Click [C] next to a chat message to select its text for copying.
- Click [L] next to a message with a detected web link to copy the URL.
- Press Ctrl+C to copy selected text, then paste it outside the game.
- In the addon window, use the [C] and [L] checkboxes to show or hide these chat buttons.
- Supported chats: /s, /raid, /bg, /g (guild), /w (incoming and outgoing whispers), and numbered channels /1 through /7.

NOTE
WoW 3.3.5a addons cannot automatically copy to the system clipboard or open a browser.

Version 1.1.5: Choose separate [C] and [L] text colors from 20 presets in the addon window. Choices persist between sessions. Dark colors may be hard to see on dark chat backgrounds.
